PrivacyTerms

Delete your account in Gamearn

  1. Open Gamearn and sign in.
  2. Open Profile, then Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Read the warning, enter DELETE, and confirm.

This permanently closes the account and initiates deletion of the associated Gamearn profile, authentication account, stored profile image, and application records handled by the deletion service. Wallet value and game history may be permanently lost.

If you cannot access the app

Email gamearnapp@gmail.com from the address associated with your account. Use the subject “Gamearn account deletion request” and include your Gamearn username. We may request information needed to verify account ownership before deletion.

Retention

We delete or anonymise account data that is no longer required. Some transaction, fraud-prevention, dispute, tax, or compliance records may be retained for the period required by applicable law. See the Privacy Policy for more information.
