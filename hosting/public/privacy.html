1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide, including your name, email address, phone number, profile information, and information supplied by Google, Facebook, or Apple when you choose social sign-in. We also process game activity, tournament participation, wallet and transaction records, device information, IP address, diagnostics, and app usage data.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, operate games and tournaments, process purchases and payouts, provide support, prevent fraud and abuse, comply with legal obligations, and improve Gamearn. We may send service messages and, where permitted, promotional communications.

3. Payments and advertising

Payments are processed by payment providers; Gamearn does not store full payment-card details. We may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob. These services may process device identifiers and activity data to deliver and measure advertising, subject to your consent and device settings.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with service providers that help operate Gamearn, including authentication, hosting, email, payments, analytics, advertising, fraud prevention, and support providers; when required by law; or when necessary to protect users, Gamearn, or others.

5. Storage and security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect information. No internet transmission or storage method is completely secure.

6. Your choices and rights

You may access or correct profile information in Gamearn and may request access, correction, or deletion by contacting us. You can permanently delete your account inside the app under Settings → Delete Account. See our Data Deletion Instructions.

7. Retention

We retain information while your account is active and as reasonably necessary to provide services, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet financial, tax, anti-money-laundering, or other legal obligations. Some transaction or compliance records may be retained after account deletion where the law requires it.

8. Children

Gamearn is intended only for people aged 18 or older, or the legal age of majority in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will post the revised effective date. Questions or privacy requests may be sent to gamearnapp@gmail.com.
